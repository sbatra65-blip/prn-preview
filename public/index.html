LMPC checklist for this template

    Minimum letter height (Rule 7, Table I)

    Display panel areaPrintedMoulded / blown
    up to 50 cm²1.0 mm2.0 mm
    50 to 100 cm²1.5 mm3.0 mm
    100 to 500 cm²2.5 mm4.0 mm
    500 to 2,500 cm²4.0 mm6.0 mm
    above 2,500 cm²6.0 mm6.0 mm

    Letter width must be at least one third of its height.

    Source: LMPC Rules 2011, as amended to 13 Feb 2026

    Declarations on every retail pack (Rule 6)

    • Name and address of manufacturer, packer or importer
    • Country of origin, for imported goods
    • Common or generic name of the commodity
    • Net quantity in standard units, or number of pieces
    • Month and year of manufacture; best before or use by for perishables
    • Retail sale price as MRP inclusive of all taxes
    • Unit sale price, unless it equals the MRP
    • Consumer care name, address, phone and email
    • Dimensions, where size matters
    Wholesale cartons follow Rule 24: maker's name and address, identity of the goods, and number of retail packs or net quantity.

    About these files

    • Same design, generated for TSPL, ZPL and PGL at 203, 300 and 600 dpi, and EPL2 at 203 dpi.
    • Prices use "Rs." because built-in printer fonts lack the ₹ sign.
    • GS1 DataMatrix prints from TSPL and ZPL; EPL2 and PGL files omit it.
    • All data is sample data. Test-print every file before production use.
