LMPC check

    What is checked

    The check reads the text on the label shown and looks for the declarations the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 ask for on retail packs (Rule 6), and compares the smallest letters with Rule 7, Table I, taking the label as the principal display panel.

    Display panel areaPrintedMoulded / blown
    up to 50 cm²1.0 mm2.0 mm
    50 to 100 cm²1.5 mm3.0 mm
    100 to 500 cm²2.5 mm4.0 mm
    500 to 2,500 cm²4.0 mm6.0 mm
    above 2,500 cm²6.0 mm6.0 mm

    Letter width must be at least one third of its height. Wholesale packs (Rule 24), exempt goods and FSSAI, drug or BIS rules need their own review.

    Source: LMPC Rules 2011, as amended to 13 Feb 2026

    About FNC1 and the special characters

    In LabelDesk you type GS1 data with the identifier in brackets, e.g. (01)08901234567890(17)271231(10)B123. The brackets are only shown in the human-readable line; they are not encoded. When the label prints, LabelDesk puts FNC1 at the start of the barcode (that is what makes it GS1-128 / GS1 DataMatrix) and a group separator (FNC1 in GS1-128, ASCII 29 <GS> in DataMatrix) after each variable-length field such as batch (10) or serial (21), but not after the last one. Fixed-length fields (00, 01, 02, dates, weights) need no separator, so the builder puts them first to keep the barcode short. For a QR, GS1 asks for a Digital Link web address instead, e.g. https://id.gs1.org/01/08901234567890/10/B123?17=271231.

    Build the data from parts

    Join fixed text, database fields, special characters (FNC1, GS…), dates and serial numbers into one string. Fields are filled from typed values or a CSV/Excel file when you print.
