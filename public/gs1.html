Getting barcodes in India

GS1 India is the only authorised body that issues barcode numbers starting with 890, India's GS1 prefix. Numbers bought from resellers or "barcode websites" are not registered to your company and can be rejected by retailers and marketplaces. GS1 India's own site warns against buying from unauthorised sources.

How registration works

  • You apply online and receive a GS1 Company Prefix, from which you make GTINs for your products.
  • The fee depends on your company's turnover, the number of barcodes you need and the subscription period. It is made up of a registration fee, a subscription fee, an interest-free refundable security deposit, and taxes.
  • Keep your GST registration, company documents and, if you are an MSME, your Udyam certificate ready.
  • Renew the subscription on time: a lapsed prefix should not stay on new packs.

The three numbers you will meet

GTIN

Global Trade Item Number: one number per product variant (pack size, flavour, colour). Printed as EAN-13 on retail packs; 14 digits on cartons.

8901234567890

SSCC

Serial Shipping Container Code: 18 digits, a new one for every pallet or parcel you ship. The one number every logistic label must carry.

389012345000000010

GLN

Global Location Number: 13 digits for a place or a legal entity, used for ship-to, ship-from and invoicing.

8901234000007

Every GTIN, SSCC and GLN ends in a check digit worked out from the other digits. Use the calculator below before you print.

How a GS1 logistic label is laid out

  • Three sections, top to bottom. A free section for the sender's name, logo or addresses; a middle section with the data in plain text for people; and a bottom section with the GS1-128 barcodes.
  • The SSCC is the only mandatory element, and it goes in the lowest barcode on the label.
  • Size. GS1 does not fix one size. A6 (105 × 148 mm) covers most needs; A5 (148 × 210 mm) leaves room for transport details. A 100 × 150 mm thermal label is the usual A6 equivalent.
  • Orientation. Barcodes on pallets and cartons run in picket-fence orientation, bars vertical, so scanners read them on a conveyor or forklift.
  • Placement. On pallets, labels go on two adjacent sides at a height scanners can reach.

Full rules: GS1 Logistic Label Guideline.

Sunrise 2027 in brief

Sunrise 2027 is GS1's industry target for shop tills to read 2D barcodes as well as linear ones by the end of 2027. Packs will carry both an EAN-13 and a 2D code during the change-over; later the 2D code alone will do.

QR code with GS1 Digital Link

The GTIN (and batch, expiry if you like) inside a web address. Tills read the GTIN; shoppers' phones open your product page. Needs a URL you control.

https://samplefoods.in/01/08901234567890/10/B2611A

GS1 India on QR codes

GS1 DataMatrix

Compact, no web address, already standard on medicines. Tills and warehouse scanners read it; ordinary phone cameras usually do not show anything useful.

(01)08901234567890(17)290930(10)PT26110

GS1 India on DataMatrix

Practical steps: keep printing the EAN-13; add the 2D code close to it on the next print run; make sure the GTIN inside both is the same; and test-scan with the retailer's scanner. Thermal transfer printers at 300 dpi give crisp small 2D codes on labels.
